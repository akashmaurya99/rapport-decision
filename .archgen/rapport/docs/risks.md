# RAPPORT — Risk Assessment

_Document 8 of the research package. Every risk names its mitigation and the task that owns it. "Residual" is the risk that remains after the mitigation — it is real, not decorative._

|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | **Capability floor.** 33M parameters is ~18× below the smallest published typed decision backbone (0.6B). RAPPORT-C may lose to TF-IDF on held-out sources. | arXiv 2611.21393 (pre-registered fine-tuning study lost to TF-IDF on 3/5 fields); arXiv 2611.23832 (frozen encoder reached 33–46% on MedMCQA) | T18 baselines are blocking; acceptance Y2 is pass/fail; RAPPORT-L (52M) is the registered fallback; the report prints the losing arm | T18, T21 | **High**, and disclosed rather than hidden |

| R3 | **Baselines are weak because the bench is weak.** MMLU/ARC measure knowledge; the model may "win" for the wrong reason. | known construct limitation of MMLU (Hendrycks et al., arXiv 2009.03300) | No aggregate score; report per family; add reward-bench-style preference OOD check; human review of 200 sampled rows | T17, T21 | Medium |

| R10 | **Abstention never fires.** The model answers everything, confidently. | arXiv 2611.22949 (TDMs rarely choose no-answer); AbstentionBench reports poor abstention (arXiv 2506.09038) | Abstention is a first-class loss with weight 1.0; the threshold derives from an explicit utility model; Y5 requires selective accuracy ≥ full accuracy +5 at 80% coverage | T11, T16, T17 | Medium |
| R11 | **Colab session interruption destroys training.** | Free tier kills sessions without warning; GPU access is not guaranteed | Checkpoints every 500 steps, atomic writes, RNG state saved, `--resume auto`; Y13 tests a simulated kill; run fits in one session | T12 | Low |
| R12 | **Silent runtime downgrade.** The session is assigned a different GPU or an older CUDA build, invalidating benchmarks. | Colab allocation is opaque | T02 probe writes capability + memory + backend to the manifest; the report refuses to compare runs with different probes | T02, T24 | Low |
| R13 | **INT8 quantization destroys the score head.** | quantization drift concentrates on low-magnitude layers (score/cosine) | Per-head drift check; score path stays FP32; export is rejected if accuracy drops >1 point | T23 | Low–Medium |
| R14 | **Uncertainty-weighted loss collapses an easy task.** | known GradNorm/σ² behaviour; the "abstain" gradient is small once accuracy is high | Floor at 0.05 and cap at 4.0; A4 compares against fixed weights; `WEIGHT_COLLAPSE` event is recorded and surfaced | T14 | Low |
| R15 | **The "512 is enough" assumption.** Constraint-heavy choices may need longer states to reason about. | unknown until measured; T19's length sweep is the test | Train at 512 but report the 768/1024 accuracy; if the drop exceeds 2 points, RoPE extension is dropped and the budget stays 512 `[honest]` | T06, T19 | Medium |
| R16 | **ARC's CC-BY-SA and SHP's undeclared license taint the artifact.** | both are the highest-quality corpora in the preference/MCQ space (see `docs/dataset-strategy.md`) | Both are quarantined in the default mixture; T07 records license status; substitution is pre-planned | T07, T13 | Low |
| R17 | **Evaluation becomes a benchmark-contamination contest.** | MMLU/HellaSwag may appear in pretraining data of the init encoder | The init encoder is an English-only embedding model; still, the report prints per-family scores and the held-out-source delta; no single "overall" figure is published | T21 | Medium |

## Ranking by expected impact on the final artifact

**R1** (capability floor) → **R9** (distillation negative) → **R2** (calibration buckets) → **R3/R17** (construct validity) → **R10** (abstention collapse). All other risks are engineering discipline problems, which the protocol mitigates.

**The honest summary:** RAPPORT is an open question in the 5M–60M regime of a newly named category. The design is defensible; the result is unknown. The plan is built so that a negative answer is discovered cheaply and reported loudly rather than shipped as a success.
